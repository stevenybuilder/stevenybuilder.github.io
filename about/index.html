About Me

I’m going after applied interpretability for world models because of the real world consequences of physical AI systems. There is increasing relevance between human neurobiological pathways and model biology in AI.

I’m currently exploring fine grained steering methods to improve robotics task outcomes off frozen JEPA models.

In the past, I’ve spent time investigating pattern separation in the hippocampus at UC Irvine’s Center for Neurobiology of Learning and Memory. The focus of my cognitive and computational neuroscience research was on human memory and attention mechanisms. Lots of MRI experiments and analysis!

During my degree, I also interned at Hoag Hospital and loved seeing the impact I could have on patients in the ICU. Currently, I work as a product manager at Experian, leading our 0 to 1 product development around AI and data science.

I also compete in martial arts! I’ve trained with UFC athletes and enjoy the constant pursuit of learning and improving.

If you’re ever in New York drop me a message! Contact links are on the bottom of the page.
